GovConUnited – The Premier Social Network for Government Contracting Professionals

The LinkedIn for Government Contractors.

GovConUnited is a specialized mobile app designed to unite the dynamic ecosystem of government contractors, procurement officers, consultants, and agency representatives. Built exclusively for the government contracting space, GovConUnited empowers professionals to connect, collaborate, and grow within a secure, compliance-aware environment.

More than just a networking platform, GovConUnited streamlines the way public and private sector professionals interact—whether you're seeking teaming partners, staying updated on federal opportunities, or showcasing past performance. The app’s core features include targeted forums, contract matchmaking, agency directories, compliance resources, and verified user profiles to foster trusted relationships and transparency.

GovConUnited is also a hub for business development, offering tools like opportunity alerts, proposal collaboration spaces, event calendars, and integrated messaging. From seasoned contractors to new 8(a) firms and procurement officers, the app helps reduce the friction in forming compliant, strategic partnerships in the federal space.

With a user-first interface and a mission to modernize how the government contracting community connects, GovConUnited is the digital home for those who build, serve, and supply the nation.

Core Features

1. Profile Types

Business profiles (with NAICS codes, certifications, past performance, capabilities)

Individual profiles (contract officers, consultants, BD professionals)

Verified government employee badges



2. Contract Opportunity Feed

Curated feed of new RFPs, RFIs, Sources Sought, etc.

Filter by agency, location, NAICS, contract type, set-aside status



3. Networking & Messaging

Direct messaging (with compliance filters for federal employees)

Follow/friend connections

Introductions (request intros from mutual connections)



4. Capability Statements & Portfolio Uploads

Attach capability statements, past performance, pitch decks

Allow multimedia (video intros, case studies, demos)





---

Business Development Tools

5. Matchmaking & Partner Discovery

AI-based suggestions for teaming/subcontracting

Match with firms by contract history, set-asides, or complementary capabilities



6. Teaming Boards

Post teaming opportunities

Collaborative proposal planning spaces

Assign roles (prime, sub, JV, etc.)



7. Past Performance Database

Showcase CPARS reviews or summary stats

Keyword searchable contract history tied to profiles



8. CRM-lite Functionality

Track agencies, contracting officers, and leads

Set reminders for follow-ups, pre-solicitation events, etc.

Topic-based communities (e.g., Construction, Cybersecurity, 8(a), Veteran-Owned)
