 🔹 About GovConUnited

1. What is GovConUnited?
   GovConUnited is a community-driven platform offering events, networking, education, and exclusive perks for professionals in the government contracting space.

2. Who is GovConUnited for?
   We serve small businesses, prime contractors, consultants, and professionals working in or entering the government contracting industry.

3. What makes GovConUnited different?
   Our focus on real community, live events, mobile engagement, and exclusive perks sets us apart from traditional consultants or educational platforms.

4. Is GovConUnited a government agency?
   No. We are a private, independent organization that supports businesses engaging with federal, state, and local government contracts.

5. How can I join GovConUnited?
   You can join by attending an event, downloading our mobile app, or subscribing to our Insider Insights platform.

---

 🔹 Events

6. What types of events do you host?
   We host mixers, summits, pitch events, networking parties, and educational workshops for GovCon professionals.

7. Are events virtual or in person?
   Most of our events are in-person, but we also offer occasional virtual sessions and replays through our platform.

8. Do I need to be a contractor to attend?
   Not necessarily—our events are open to anyone interested in the GovCon space, including vendors, consultants, and newcomers.

9. How do I find upcoming events?
   Visit [govconunited.com/events](https://govconunited.com/events) or check our mobile app for upcoming events and registration links.

10. Are events only in Washington, D.C.?
    While D.C. is our primary hub, we occasionally host events in other major cities with a strong GovCon presence.

---

 🔹 Mobile App

11. What is the GovConUnited app?
    Our mobile app helps you connect with other contractors, access exclusive perks, register for events, and stay plugged into GovCon news.

12. Is the app free?
    Yes, the base version is free. Members receive added benefits through premium tiers.

13. What platforms is the app available on?
    The GovConUnited app is available on both iOS and Android.

14. Can I register for events on the app?
    Yes. You can RSVP, get event details, and receive exclusive offers directly from the app.

15. What perks come with the app?
    Event discounts, early access to content, community networking, and exclusive merch drops.

 🔹 Community & Networking

16. How do I connect with other members?
    Through in-person events, private Slack & WhatsApp groups, and inside the mobile app.

17. Is there a cost to join the community?
    Basic community access is free. Premium tiers offer more benefits like priority invites and access to paid content.

18. Can I promote my business in the community?
    Yes—members can share their services, contracts won, job openings, and more (respectfully and within community guidelines).

19. Is this for experienced contractors only?
    No—new and aspiring contractors are welcome and encouraged to join and learn.

20. Are there mentorship opportunities?
    Yes. We regularly host mentor matching sessions and offer guidance through our GovTalk series.

    GovTalk is our podcast and video series featuring interviews, contract tips, success stories, and insider perspectives from the GovCon world.

22. Where can I listen to GovTalk?
