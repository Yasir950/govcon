About | Video to the left

GovConUnited™ is redefining the way government contractors connect, learn, and grow. As a premier events company at the heart of the government contracting ecosystem, we bring together industry professionals, small businesses, consultants, and agency insiders through dynamic, in-person and virtual experiences that spark collaboration and unlock opportunity. Whether you're just entering the government contracting marketplace or are a seasoned player, GovConUnited™ provides the access and energy you need to thrive in today’s competitive contracting landscape.

But we don’t stop at the ballroom and digital doors. Our powerful mobile application extends the networking potential far beyond our events, allowing contractors from across the globe to connect, share insights, and build relationships in real time. Designed for today’s on-the-move professionals, the GovConUnited™ app delivers exclusive perks, early event access, and a digital community unlike any other in the industry.

(Learn More) button

Founded in 2023 by Romane Orlando Robb, GovConUnited™ is backed by a strong corporate foundation. It operates under GovCon IP, LLC, which is wholly owned by Robb Industrial Corporation, a diversified business group with deep roots in innovation and infrastructure. This strategic structure ensures that GovConUnited™ remains well-capitalized, agile, and positioned for long-term growth across the government services sector.

Our events are co-produced by Robb Creative Group Corporation/Planet27, bringing a level of polish, creativity, and impact that sets the GovConUnited™ experience apart. From high-energy mixers to targeted summits, every detail is curated to deliver maximum value for our attendees. Whether you’re looking to land your first contract, scale your GovCon brand, or simply stay ahead of the curve, GovConUnited™ is where your government contracting journey finds its community.
